Length & Confidence Model

Accounting for Sentinel-1 IW impulse response broadening, estimated hull length and detection confidence are derived from radar cross-section:

L_est = sqrt(area_m2 * 4.2)
Conf = 0.65 * norm(vv_db) + 0.35 * norm(area_m2)
Temporal AIS Interpolation

Because AIS broadcasts asynchronously (every 2 sec to 6 min), comparing raw pings to SAR causes false dark alerts. Team Maya interpolates every track to $t_{\text{SAR}}$:

alpha = (t_SAR - t_1) / (t_2 - t_1)
P(t_SAR) = P_1 + alpha * (P_2 - P_1)
Azimuth Doppler Shift Allowance

A radial velocity $v_r$ shifts a moving ship in the SAR azimuth direction by $\Delta x = (R / V_{\text{sat}}) v_r$ (typically 150–450 m), motivating our default 500 m matching buffer.

Default Buffer = 500 m (Adjustable 100-2000 m)

2. Honest Physical & Operational Limitations (Team Maya Judge Briefing)

1. Spatial Resolution Floor (< 15–20 m Missed) SENSOR PHYSICS

Sentinel-1 IW mode has a ~20×22 m spatial resolution (10 m pixel spacing). Small wooden dhows, fiberglass skiffs, and artisanal fishing boats below 15–20 m produce weak radar cross-sections (RCS) indistinguishable from background Bragg sea scatter, especially at high incidence angles.

2. Wind/Wave Bragg Clutter & Azimuth Ambiguities FALSE ALARMS

Strong Shamal winds (>10 m/s) in the Arabian Gulf generate whitecaps, steep breaking waves, and internal wave wakes that elevate VV backscatter and trigger false alarms. Bright coastal cranes at Jebel Ali can also cause ghost azimuth ambiguities kilometers offshore.

3. AIS Spoofing & Reception Gaps RF & SPOOFING

An unmatched SAR detection is not automatically illicit. In dense hubs like Jebel Ali and the Strait of Hormuz, VHF slot collisions degrade satellite AIS reception. Conversely, bad actors may broadcast spoofed GNSS coordinates miles away from their true physical hull position.

4. Orbital Revisit Interval (Days Apart, Not Continuous) TEMPORAL GAP

Sun-synchronous SAR satellites image the Strait of Hormuz only every 6 to 12 days per orbit track. SAR provides high-confidence wide-area snapshots to cue commercial X-band SAR tasking (ICEYE, Capella), RF geolocation, or coastal patrol aircraft.
